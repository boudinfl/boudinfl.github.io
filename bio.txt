Florian Boudin is an Associate Professor of Computer Science at Nantes Université, where he co-leads the TALN research group at LS2N, and is currently on secondment at Inria. His research lies at the intersection of Natural Language Processing and Information Retrieval, with a focus on scholarly documents. He is interested in how language models can help researchers write and revise papers, check that scientific claims are supported by evidence, and find their way through an ever-growing literature. His work has been published in venues such as ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, COLING and LREC.
